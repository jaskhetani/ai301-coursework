# Scope: where your issue lives, and the rules of the room

## Where your issue lives

Only issues in the course's Path Review repository are in scope this week:

- Repo: `codepath/pathreview-ai301-fa26-s1`

Your plan must belong to the issue you reproduced in week 2 (or the
house issue the instructor routed you to). Do not grade plan packages
for issues in any other repository.

## Path Review house rules

- **A classmate's plan comment does not block yours.** Post your own
  plan in your own words, built from your own reproduction.
- **Never piggyback a plan.** "Same approach as above" is not a plan.
- **Branch on your own fork, named `fix/<issue-number>-<slug>`.** Push
  the branch to your fork; do not push to the shared repository.
- **Credit attaches to the PR you open in week 4.** A shared issue
  does not reduce credit.
